Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and keep the Bharath Bazar store locator simple and anonymous. This app is designed to help customers quickly find nearby product locations and understand which products people search for most often.

What data we collect

We do not collect personal identifiers such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, payment details, or customer account information. We only collect anonymous technical and usage data that is needed to improve product discovery:

  • Anonymous browser/device information such as device type, operating system, browser, screen size, and a generated anonymous device identifier.
  • Anonymous search queries entered in the app, such as product names or local language terms.
  • Basic usage metadata such as visit counts and the last time the app was used.

How we use the data

The anonymous data is used to:

  • Understand what items customers search for most often.
  • Improve search relevance and product discovery.
  • Measure overall usage and device trends without identifying individuals.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information. We do not store names, addresses, account details, or contact information. We do not profile users as individuals.

Data retention

Anonymous usage logs are kept only as long as needed for product and search analysis. We do not retain personal records in this app.

Third-party tools

This app may use third-party services to host the site and collect anonymous analytics. The data sent to those services is limited to anonymous device and search metadata and does not include direct personal information. The current service used for anonymous tracking is Google Forms for basic analytics collection.

Your choices

You can accept or reject anonymous analytics tracking from the consent banner shown on first visit. If you reject, the app continues to work normally for product search without sending anonymous analytics.
