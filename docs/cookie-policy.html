Cookie Policy

This site uses a minimal set of browser storage to keep the app working and to support anonymous analytics when you choose to accept tracking. We do not use cookies for advertising or personal profiling.

Most browser storage entries remain until they are removed by the browser or until the app resets them. Consent is kept until you change your preference. Anonymous analytics data is retained only for analysis and is not linked to personal identity.

How to disable or reject cookies

You can reject tracking at any time using the consent banner or via browser settings. If you reject, the app will continue to work for product search without anonymous analytics collection. You may also clear local browser storage from your browser settings.
